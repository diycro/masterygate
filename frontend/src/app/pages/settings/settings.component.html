Your API key, your browser, your data

This app runs entirely in your browser. Curriculum content ships with the app; your progress lives only in this browser's local storage; and grading / "generate more questions" / mock interviews call your own Groq, OpenAI, or Anthropic API key directly from your browser. The key is never sent anywhere except straight to that provider.

LLM provider

Get a key from console.groq.com/keys platform.openai.com/api-keys console.anthropic.com/settings/keys — stored only in localStorage on this device. Groq's free tier needs no card and no billing setup.

Local progress

Gate passes, course completion, resource checkmarks, and mock-interview history all live in this browser only. Clearing your browser's site data (or a different browser/device) starts fresh.
